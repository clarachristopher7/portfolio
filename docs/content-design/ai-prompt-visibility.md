# AI prompt logging

<p className="hub-sub">Admins can keep a log of what employees type into AI tools. Turning it on needed a second setting on a different screen, and without it the log stayed empty.</p>

<dl className="ctx">
  <div><dt>The product</dt><dd>Cloud Secure Edge, a SonicWall service that controls which employees and devices can reach a company’s apps and websites.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>Who uses it</dt><dd>IT admins.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>What they’re doing</dt><dd>Turning on a log of what employees type into AI tools like ChatGPT, to see what they share with them. For the log to work, the product must be allowed to read encrypted web traffic, a setting that lives on a separate screen.</dd></div>
  <div><dt>My part</dt><dd>With the product designer, I proposed setting the feature up before switching it on, with the missing setting on the same screen.</dd></div>
</dl>
